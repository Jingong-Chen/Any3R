Twenty views of one street, each rendered through one of eight camera models (pinhole, Brown-Conrady, equidistant, Kannala-Brandt, MEI, double sphere, equirectangular window, full equirectangular) from PanoCity panoramas. This is the input of the paper's teaser figure.

Source: PanoCity (Guo et al., PanoVGGT, 2026), <https://huggingface.co/datasets/YijingGuo/PanoCity>, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Images were resampled for this example; no other changes.
